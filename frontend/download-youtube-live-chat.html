Complete Guide to Downloading YouTube Live Chat Replays with Timestamps

YouTube live streaming has evolved into the central hub for gaming tournaments, esports, VTuber events, and interactive community broadcasts. While standard YouTube video comments are easily visible, live chat replay messages are stored as time-synchronized dynamic event streams. Once a live stream concludes, traditional browser copy-pasting fails to capture thousands of rapid-fire messages, Super Chat donation amounts, or membership milestones.

How YouTube Live Chat Replay Works Under the Hood

During an active broadcast, YouTube streams chat messages in real time using internal polling continuations. When the broadcast ends and is archived as a Video on Demand (VOD), YouTube processes the chat replay into timestamped JSON chunks mapped directly to the video's relative playback timeline.

💡 Processing Delay Note: YouTube typically requires between 30 minutes to 2 hours after a live stream ends to process and finalize the official Live Chat Replay. Ensure that "Chat replay" was not manually turned off in the creator's YouTube Studio settings.

Key Data Fields Extracted in TubeMetrix Live Chat Logs

  • Relative Video Timestamp: Exact stream offset (e.g. 01:24:18) synchronizing the chat message with on-screen gameplay or tournament moments.
  • Author Details: Commenter display name, permanent channel ID, verified checkmarks, and member milestone badges.
  • Monetization Flags: Super Chat donation amounts, currency type, Super Sticker identifiers, and YouTube Jewels gift totals.
  • Message Content: Full raw text, custom channel emojis, and message event categorization.

Why Content Creators & Editors Rely on Chat Replay Downloads

Frequently Asked Questions About YouTube Live Chat Replay

Why is live chat replay unavailable on some past YouTube streams?

Live chat replay is unavailable if the streamer disabled "Allow chat replay" in their YouTube Studio stream settings, if YouTube is still processing the replay (usually takes 30-120 minutes post-stream), or if the VOD was set to private or age-restricted.

Does TubeMetrix capture Super Chat donations and currency values?

Yes! Every Super Chat, Super Sticker, and Membership milestone is parsed with its exact original currency and donation amount. TubeMetrix also calculates net creator take-home after YouTube's 30% revenue share.

How are timestamps formatted in Excel and CSV exports?

Timestamps are exported in both relative stream playback format (HH:MM:SS from stream start) and absolute ISO UTC timestamp format, ensuring clean synchronization in Excel or Python.

Can I download live chats from ongoing live broadcasts?

TubeMetrix is optimized for completed live stream VODs and replays to guarantee 100% transcript completeness. For active ongoing streams, chats can be extracted once the stream session concludes.

What export formats are supported?

You can export your complete chat replay dataset to Microsoft Excel (.xlsx), Comma-Separated Values (.csv), Plain Text (.txt), or structured JSON for developer pipelines.
