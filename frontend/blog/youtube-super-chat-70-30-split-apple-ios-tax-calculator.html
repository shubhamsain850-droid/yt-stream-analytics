The YouTube Super Chat 70/30 Split & Apple iOS Tax Trap: Net Payouts Explained

Ask almost any live streamer how much YouTube takes from Super Chats, Super Stickers, and Channel Memberships, and they will give the standard headline answer: "YouTube takes 30%, and creators keep 70%."

While technically true on desktop web browsers and Android, thousands of streamers are shocked when checking their monthly AdSense payouts to discover they received nearly half of what popped up on screen during their stream. Why? Because of Apple's App Store In-App Purchase commission.

1. YouTube's Official Terms on "Supers"

Under YouTube's official Partner Program terms for Fan Funding, creators receive 70% of recognized revenue after local sales taxes and third-party app store fees are subtracted. Google covers standard web credit card processing charges on desktop and Android browsers—meaning on a $100 desktop Super Chat, you genuinely receive $70.

2. The Hidden Apple iOS 30% Fee (The 51% Effective Deduction)

When a viewer sends a Super Chat or buys a Channel Membership from inside the official YouTube app on an iPhone or iPad (iOS), Apple routes the transaction through Apple In-App Purchases and deducts its mandatory 30% platform cut before Google ever calculates creator revenue sharing.

⚠️ The Double Deduction Formula ($100 iOS Example)

1. Viewer sends $100.00 on iPhone → 2. Apple deducts 30% (-$30.00), leaving $70.00 → 3. YouTube takes 30% of that remaining $70.00 (-$21.00) → 4. Creator Net Payout = $49.00 (Only 49%)!

3. Desktop / Android vs. Apple iOS Comparison ($100 Gross)

Remaining Revenue Base $100.00 $70.00
YouTube Cut (30% of Base) -$30.00 -$21.00
Net Creator Take-Home $70.00 (70%) $49.00 (49%)

How to Spot iOS Donations in Chat Logs: Because Apple enforces fixed App Store price tiers, iPhone Super Chats almost always show odd decimal amounts ending in .99 (such as $4.99, $9.99, $19.99, or ₹179/₹449 in India), whereas web donors send clean round numbers like $5.00 or $10.00.

4. How YouTube Jewels & Rubies Compare

In addition to Super Chats, YouTube introduced vertical live-stream Gifts powered by Jewels & Rubies. Viewers buy prepaid Jewels bundles, and when they send animated gifts, creators earn Rubies where 1 Ruby pays creators $0.01 USD (2 Jewels = 1 Ruby).
