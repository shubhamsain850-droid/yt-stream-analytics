Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about YouTube Jewels, Rubies, and payouts.

How much is 1 YouTube Jewel worth to creators? +

YouTube uses a fixed 2:1 ratio: 2 Jewels = 1 Ruby = $0.01 USD (~₹0.43 INR net to creator). Viewers purchase retail Jewels bundles at ~₹1.20 per Jewel.

When and how does YouTube pay creators for Jewels? +

Rubies earnings are disbursed monthly via your linked Google AdSense account between the 21st and 26th, subject to the standard AdSense threshold ($100 USD or ₹7,000 INR).

Are YouTube Jewels available on horizontal live streams? +

No, Jewels and virtual gifts are exclusive to vertical mobile live streams (Shorts feed). Jewels and virtual gifts can only be sent on vertical live streams in the YouTube mobile app.

Can TubeMetrix track Jewels sent during past live stream archives? +

Yes! Paste any public stream URL to scan the chat replay, itemize every gift, and calculate total Jewels and net creator payout.

Do I need to sign in or pay to use this calculator? +
