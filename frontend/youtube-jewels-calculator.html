Frequently Asked Questions: YouTube Jewels & Rubies

What is the official conversion formula from YouTube Jewels to Rubies? +

Under YouTube's official partner policy, 2 Jewels = 1 Ruby. When a viewer sends a virtual gift costing 100 Jewels, the creator receives exactly 50 Rubies in their creator analytics studio.

How much does YouTube pay creators for 1 Ruby in INR and USD? +

YouTube pays creators a fixed rate of $0.01 USD per Ruby. In Indian Rupees (INR), 1 Ruby converts to approximately ₹0.84 to ₹0.87 INR based on real-time forex rates. This means each Jewel earned yields approximately ₹0.42 INR ($0.005 USD) net take-home for the creator.

How much do viewers spend to buy Jewels bundles in India? +

Viewers purchase Jewels bundles through Google Play in-app purchases. In India, retail pricing is approximately ₹1.20 INR per Jewel (for instance, ~₹12 for 10 Jewels, ~₹120 for 100 Jewels). In the United States, popular bundles start at $9.99 for 700 Jewels.

When and how do creators receive payments for Rubies? +

Rubies earnings are added to your YouTube Creator Studio monthly revenue pool and paid out via your linked Google AdSense account between the 21st and 26th of each month, provided your total earnings meet the AdSense minimum payout threshold ($100 USD or ₹7,000 INR).

Are YouTube Jewels available on horizontal live streams? +

Virtual gifts and Jewels are optimized specifically for vertical livestreams appearing in the YouTube Shorts feed on mobile devices. Standard horizontal streams use Super Chats and Super Stickers.
