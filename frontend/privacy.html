Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026
Overview: TubeMetrix ("we", "our", or "us") provides YouTube creator tools, analytics, and live stream data auditing. We respect your privacy and only process the minimal information necessary to deliver our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Google Identity Information: When you sign in via Google One-Tap or OAuth, Google provides us with your public profile name, verified email address, and avatar URL. We do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Usage & Quota Data: We store local session identifiers and daily audit counters to manage free-tier rate limits and verify active PRO subscriptions.
  • Public YouTube Data: YouTube URLs and channel IDs you submit are processed strictly to query public video metadata, comments, and Super Chat data via public APIs.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Authenticate your account and maintain your session.
  • Track and enforce fair usage limits (e.g. daily free audit quotas).
  • Process bulk stream exports and provide access to premium analytics features.
  • Provide technical support and detect fraudulent or automated abuse.

3. Third-Party Services & APIs

TubeMetrix integrates with trusted third-party providers:

  • Google APIs & Google Identity Services: Used for seamless user authentication. Your use of Google Sign-In is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube: All YouTube metrics are sourced publicly. TubeMetrix is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google LLC.
  • Payment Gateways (Lemon Squeezy / Paddle): When purchasing PRO Day Passes or subscriptions, payment processing is handled securely by our Merchant of Record. We never store credit card numbers or banking credentials.

4. Data Retention & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Authentication tokens are handled securely with industry-standard encryption. You may request account deletion at any time by contacting our support team.
