Frequently Asked Questions

How to download YouTube live chat replay? +

Paste your YouTube live stream URL or Video ID into the Single Live Chat Mode tab. TubeMetrix fetches the complete chat replay with timestamps, Superchats, Memberships, and regular messages — then export to CSV, Excel, or TXT.

Can I export YouTube comments to Excel or CSV? +

Yes! Use Option D (Video Comments) to fetch all comments from any YouTube video. Export to Excel (.xls), CSV, TXT, or JSON with one click. Works with regular videos, Shorts, and live stream replays.

What are the free limits and credit rates for live chat exports? +

Single Stream mode includes 2 one-time free runs as a Guest and 3 free runs per week after Google Sign-In (for single chat replays or up to 1,000 video comments). Custom URL Batch Export and Multi Live Chat Export (Date Range) use never-expiring prepaid Credits (Global packs from $0.049/1k chats; India UPI from ₹49; 1 Credit = 1,000 chats).

How to filter Superchats from live chat logs? +

After downloading, open the in-browser Chat Viewer and use the Event Type filter to show only Superchats. Set a minimum Superchat amount filter (e.g. ₹100+, ₹1000+) and sort by highest value to find your top supporters.

Does TubeMetrix work with YouTube Shorts comments? +

Yes! TubeMetrix can download comments from YouTube Shorts, regular long-form videos, and live stream replays. Just paste the YouTube Shorts URL or video ID into the Video Comments (Option D) tab.
