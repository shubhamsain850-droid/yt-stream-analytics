Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is TubeMetrix channel earnings estimate? +

TubeMetrix uses industry-standard CPM benchmarks ($0.25–$4.00 per 1,000 views) adjusted for channel niche, geographic audience location, and content type. These are estimated ranges based on public benchmarks — actual earnings from YouTube Studio may vary.

What is a SocialBlade grade and how is it calculated? +

SocialBlade grade (A++ to F) rates a YouTube channel's growth velocity — combining subscriber growth rate, view velocity, and momentum. TubeMetrix calculates an equivalent grade using the same methodology based on a channel's 30-day growth data.

How to check YouTube channel statistics without logging in? +

Simply type any YouTube channel name, @handle, or paste the channel URL into TubeMetrix's search bar. No login or YouTube account required. The tool fetches public channel statistics in seconds.

Is TubeMetrix a free SocialBlade alternative? +

Yes! TubeMetrix is completely free. It shows channel statistics, subscriber count, total views, SocialBlade-style grade, global rank, country rank, category rank, 30-day growth, and a daily metrics table — all without any sign-up or payment.

How to find YouTube channel daily view count history? +

Search any channel on TubeMetrix and scroll to the Daily Channel Metrics table. It shows daily view counts, subscriber gains, cumulative views, and estimated daily earnings for the last 14 or 30 days — just like SocialBlade but completely free.
