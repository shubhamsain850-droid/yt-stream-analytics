Frequently Asked Questions

How much do YouTubers earn from Super Chats? +

YouTubers keep approximately 70% of Super Chat revenue after YouTube's 30% platform cut. For example, a ₹100 Super Chat earns the creator roughly ₹70. TubeMetrix calculates exact net creator earnings after all fees for every stream automatically.

How to calculate YouTube live stream revenue? +

Simply paste your YouTube live stream URL or Video ID into TubeMetrix. The tool fetches all Super Chats, Memberships, and YouTube Jewels gifts and calculates total gross and net creator earnings with live forex conversion to your chosen currency.

What are YouTube Jewels and how much are they worth? +

YouTube Jewels (also called YouTube Gifts) are virtual items sent during live streams, primarily in India. The gross rate is ₹1.20 per jewel, and after YouTube's cut the creator receives approximately ₹0.42 per jewel. TubeMetrix automatically converts all jewel gifts to INR, USD, EUR or any currency.

Can I export Super Chat data to Excel or CSV? +

Yes! TubeMetrix lets you export full Super Chat logs, Membership records, and Jewels data to Excel (.xls), CSV, and formatted Notepad (.txt) with one click. The exported files are formatted for accountants, tax records, and YouTube editors.

Does TubeMetrix support bulk export for multiple live streams? +

Yes! TubeMetrix has three modes: (A) Single Stream — paste one URL, (B) Channel & Date Range (PRO) — automatically fetches all streams from a channel within a date window, and (C) Custom URL Batch — add up to 3 stream links free daily, or unlock an unlimited batch queue with PRO.
